Getting started+

On first launch, create a PIN (4–12 digits) on the on-screen keypad and confirm it. You'll see a one-time warning: there is no PIN recovery.

After that, the same PIN screen decides what you see:

  • Correct PIN → your real session, exactly as you left it.
  • Any other PIN → a brand-new empty session, no error shown.
Tabs & navigating+

The address bar doubles as a search box. Icons around it: a lock/magnifying-glass for connection security, a star to bookmark, and a download arrow that lights up when video is found on the page.

The bottom toolbar has back/forward, a + for a new tab, and a numbered square that opens the tab overview.

Bookmarks & history+

Open the ••• menu for Bookmarks and History. A blank tab also shows a speed-dial grid of your bookmarks.

Both belong to your real session only — the decoy session never records any.

The decoy session, explained+

It's a privacy safety net: if someone asks to look through your phone, entering any PIN other than your real one opens a completely fresh, empty browser instead of an error.

  • Telling them apart: the lock icon (bottom-right of the toolbar) is green in your real session, red in a decoy.
  • Nothing carries over: decoy activity disappears the moment you lock the app again — it can't be merged into your real session.
  • Best-effort only: this hides activity from casual inspection, not from a determined technical or forensic search. See Terms §8.
Downloads & the video grabber+

Tap the address-bar download icon to see video found on the page. The first time, you'll see a one-time reminder: only save video you own or have permission to keep — you're responsible for copyright and each site's terms.

  • Direct files download in one tap; HLS streams let you pick a quality first.
  • Supported downloads can be paused and resumed.
  • Everything lands in ••• → Downloads: play in-app, export via the share icon, or delete.

Files stay in the app's private storage, never your Photos library, until you export them. Streaming sites with DRM-protected players are deliberately not supported.

Privacy & content controls+

••• → Settings covers:

  • Auto-lock — 1, 3, 5, 10 minutes, or Never.
  • Floating lock button — an optional draggable button that locks instantly on tap.
  • Block autoplay and block ads & trackers — on by default.
  • Search engine — DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Brave Search or Google.
  • Theme — System, Light or Dark.

Always on, no setting needed: the app hides its content from the iOS app switcher the instant it's backgrounded, and the toolbar lock icon locks the app immediately on tap.

Managing your data+
  • Clear Cache & Cookies — removes site data (you'll be signed out), keeps history/bookmarks/downloads.
  • Delete Everything — removes downloads, history, bookmarks, tabs and site data in one step. Your PIN is kept.

Deleting the app itself erases everything permanently, including the PIN.

Pro features+

An optional subscription (monthly or yearly, billed through the App Store) unlocks Find in Page and Save Page as PDF. Locked items show a small lock icon in the ••• menu until you subscribe.

Manage or cancel any time in your device's App Store settings, or tap Restore Purchases in Settings if you subscribed on another device.

FAQ+

I forgot my PIN — how do I get back in?

You can't. There's no recovery, backdoor or reset by design. Deleting and reinstalling the app is the only way forward, and it erases everything.

How do I know which session I'm in?

The lock icon in the toolbar: green is real, red is decoy.

Does the app work offline?

No — it's a browser, so it needs a connection to load anything.

Can I recover a deleted download or cleared history?

No. Deletions are immediate and permanent.

Is my browsing completely anonymous?

No. Sites and your network/ISP can still see your requests. The app blocks common ad/tracker domains and runs no analytics of its own, but it isn't a VPN or Tor.
